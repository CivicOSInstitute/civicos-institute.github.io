# Minimum Viable Equity Access Policy (Draft)

Purpose:
Maintain price integrity while providing a documented access path for under-resourced schools/programs.

Policy (Phase 0):
- Standard price remains $179.
- Reduced-cost access may be granted for qualifying under-resourced institutions.
- Requests reviewed case-by-case with simple documentation.

Criteria (initial):
- Public/under-resourced status
- Demonstrated student access need
- Intended instructional deployment

Process:
1. Submit request via direct email
2. Institute reviews within 5 business days
3. Written decision and terms issued

Note:
This is a minimum viable policy for Phase 0 and may be refined after demand/operational review.
