# End-of-March Grant Readiness Plan (West Palm Beach / Florida)

## Goal
Have 5 grant opportunities fully submission-ready by **March 31, 2026**.

## Priority Grants (Tier 1)
1. Walmart Foundation — Spark Good Local Grants
2. Community Foundation for Palm Beach & Martin Counties
3. Wells Fargo — Palm Beach Community Trust Fund
4. Impact 100 Palm Beach County
5. United Way of Palm Beach County grant pathways

## Timeline

### Week 1 (Mar 4–Mar 10) — Qualification + Positioning
- Confirm each grant’s current cycle, deadline, and eligibility
- Confirm 501(c)(3) vs fiscal sponsor requirements
- Finalize one-paragraph CivicOS positioning statement per funder
- Draft eligibility inquiry email for each funder

### Week 2 (Mar 11–Mar 17) — Core Application Packet
- Finalize org one-pager
- Finalize project narrative (problem, intervention, outcomes)
- Finalize baseline budget + budget narrative
- Prepare legal/admin packet (EIN/501(c)(3)/fiscal sponsor docs)

### Week 3 (Mar 18–Mar 24) — Grant-Specific Customization
- Tailor narrative to each funder’s language and priorities
- Insert requested metrics/KPIs per funder
- Build final submission checklist per grant
- Internal review pass for completeness and consistency

### Week 4 (Mar 25–Mar 31) — Submission Window
- Submit grants with near deadlines first
- Confirm portal receipt / confirmation emails
- Log follow-up dates and expected response windows
- Queue second-wave opportunities for April

## Success Criteria by Mar 31
- 5/5 opportunities fully screened and go/no-go confirmed
- At least 3 complete submissions sent (target: 5)
- All submissions tracked in one status sheet with deadlines and confirmation IDs

## Operating Notes
- Use language matching each funder’s stated priorities
- Lead with local impact in West Palm Beach + measurable outcomes
- Avoid submitting to any program with unresolved eligibility risks
