## What We Do
CivicOS Institute builds practical civic participation pathways that help residents and community groups move from intent to implementation. We combine local engagement programming, operational support tools, and measurable reporting.

## Why It Matters
Communities face a participation gap: people care about local issues but lack clear, supported structures for sustained civic action. Small and grassroots groups often have mission clarity but limited implementation capacity.

## Our Model
1. **Civic Participation Activation** — structured opportunities for local action  
2. **Community Capacity Infrastructure** — workflows/tools that improve execution  
3. **Measurement Loop** — KPI tracking + quarterly performance reviews

## 12-Month Outcomes (Targets)

- 8+ partner organizations engaged
- 50+ documented civic actions
- 4/4 quarterly reports delivered on time
