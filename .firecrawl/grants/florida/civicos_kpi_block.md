# CivicOS Institute — Grant KPI Block (Insert into Applications)

## Core KPI Set (12 months)

### Reach & Participation
1. **Residents Engaged**
   - Target: **300+** unique residents reached through CivicOS-supported activities
2. **Active Participants**
   - Target: **120+** residents participating in at least one structured civic action/workshop/session
3. **Repeat Participation Rate**
   - Target: **40%+** of participants engage in two or more activities

### Community Capacity
4. **Community Initiatives Supported**
   - Target: **12+** local initiatives/groups receive practical support (workflows, facilitation, coordination)
5. **Partner Organizations Activated**
   - Target: **8+** partner organizations/groups engaged in delivery or collaboration
6. **Capacity Assets Deployed**
   - Target: **20+** reusable templates/resources/workflows adopted by community participants

### Implementation & Outcomes
7. **Civic Actions Completed**
   - Target: **50+** documented civic actions (events, campaigns, issue-based participation activities)
8. **Project Completion Rate**
   - Target: **75%+** of supported initiatives complete planned milestone scope
9. **Participant-Reported Value**
   - Target: **80%+** report increased ability/confidence to engage in local civic activity

### Equity & Access
10. **Underserved Community Inclusion**
    - Target: At least **50%** of participants from priority/underserved communities (as locally defined)
11. **Accessibility Compliance**
    - Target: **100%** of public-facing sessions include baseline accessibility supports (clear language, online/offline options where feasible)

### Learning & Accountability
12. **Reporting Cadence**
    - Target: Quarterly performance summary delivered on time (**4/4 reports**)
13. **Data Quality Standard**
    - Target: **95%+** of KPI entries complete and verifiable
14. **Continuous Improvement Actions**
    - Target: **1+** documented program improvement implemented per quarter based on data/feedback

---

## Measurement Methods
- Attendance/registration logs
- Partner activity reports
- Program implementation tracker
- Pre/post participant pulse surveys
- Quarterly outcome review and narrative synthesis

## Suggested Grant-Ready Outcome Statement
"Within 12 months, CivicOS will engage at least 300 residents, support at least 12 community-led initiatives, and document measurable gains in participation, civic capacity, and implementation outcomes through a transparent quarterly reporting framework."
