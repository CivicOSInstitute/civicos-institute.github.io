## Standard Application Packet Checklist
- 1-page organization overview (mission, geography, beneficiaries)
- 1-page project narrative (problem, intervention, expected outcomes)
- Line-item budget + budget narrative
- Legal status docs: IRS letter OR fiscal sponsor agreement
- Board/staff list + key bios
- Measurement plan (3-5 KPIs, reporting cadence)
- Optional: letters of support from local partners
