# CivicOS Institute Mission and Scope Brief

_Status: Nonprofit in formation_

## Mission
CivicOS Institute exists to improve how residents access essential public services by reducing friction, delays, and digital barriers, especially for families and communities that are often underserved.

## Scope (Current)
CivicOS is being developed as one unified cross-agency system with shared identity, workflow, auditability, and resident-facing transparency.

### Initial pilot sequence
1. **Permits Pilot**
   - Fence
   - Shed
   - Window replacement
   - Garage door replacement
2. **311 Pilot**
   - Intake
   - Routing
   - SLA tracking and escalation
   - Closure evidence
3. **Community Services Pilot**
   - Unified intake for rent, power, and water assistance
   - Consent-based cross-agency sharing

## Delivery Principles
- Unified system, not disconnected tools
- Human-in-the-loop approvals for high-impact decisions
- Privacy, consent, and auditability by default
- Accessibility-first design (mobile, low-bandwidth, multilingual, assisted channels)
- Measurable outcomes and phased scale-up

## Current Phase
Pre-formation execution and pilot preparation, with transparent status and governance discipline.
