Dear Friends,

Time is the scarcest human resource. It cannot be recovered.

Broken civic systems consume the most time from people with the least margin: working families, caregivers, and residents navigating multiple services under pressure.

That is a structural inequity.

Public-interest intelligence should reduce that burden by cutting cycle times, reducing repeated paperwork, improving routing to help, and increasing status transparency.

Every unresolved workflow and preventable delay is a tax on human lives.

In the next 1,000 days, civic technology should be judged by one standard first: did it give people meaningful time back?

With resolve,
Nicholas A. Cerbone
